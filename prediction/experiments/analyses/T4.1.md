## Decision rule & verdict

Pre-stated rule: winner = best mean MDA subject to mean NRMSE not >10% worse than the best mean NRMSE across the six combos; ties (≤1e−4 MDA) broken by simpler model.

- Best combo NRMSE = 0.001073 (C-enet) → eligibility threshold 0.001180.
- **A-enet (best MDA 0.6490, NRMSE 0.001190) misses eligibility by a hair** (ratio 1.109 > 1.10) — an honest letter-of-the-rule exclusion worth flagging to the researcher: it is simultaneously the closest any model has come to persistence MDA (paired ΔMDA only −0.0017) and the clearest evidence of directional signal beyond momentum inside the linear family (+0.0155 vs ridge α=1, 4/5 folds).
- Among eligible {B-enet, C-enet}: B-enet wins MDA (0.5981 vs 0.5960) outright → **winner = B-enet (ElasticNet on base lag-1 + OWN block)**, MDA 0.5981 / NMSE 1.37e-06 / NRMSE 0.00107 / NMAE 6.77e-04. Its point errors land IN the persistence class — NRMSE actually BELOW persistence (0.00107 vs 0.00113) — while MDA stays 0.079 below.

## Interpretation

1. **No linear model beats persistence direction** on these certified sets (standing multi-metric bar unmet by every combo). Persistence remains the bar going into T4.2 (trees).
2. **ElasticNet's sparsity decisively beats plain Ridge here** — every enet combo dominates its ridge_cv twin on BOTH metrics; inner CV pushes Ridge to large alphas (3–10) yet pooled-Ridge-on-176-correlated-lags stays at the α=1 reference level or worse, while ElasticNet's l1 selection (chosen l1_ratio drifts to 0.95 in later folds) both denoises directions (best MDA) and, combined with the OWN shrinkage block, reaches persistence-class point errors.
3. The OWN block is again a point-error lever, not a direction lever: B/C-enet cut NRMSE ~10–12% below A-enet while losing ~0.05 MDA — consistent with the T3.3-certified story.
4. k=5 spatial adds nothing once tuning is honest (C ≈ B everywhere; the T3.1 "+0.0100 marginal keep" does not survive as a measurable gain under tuned linear vehicles).
