## 2. The temporal-feature experiment

### Feature construction (STRICTLY CAUSAL)

All engineered features are backward-looking transforms computed ONCE from the train split (1981-01→2022-03, 7792 rows × 176 base columns, 24 CMAs); each row at month t uses values up to t−1 only — the same convention as upstream `total_lag_1`. Rolling windows END at t−1 (`rolling(w, min_periods=6).mean/std().shift(1)`). Residual NaNs (each CMA's first months) filled causally: per-CMA ffill → same-month cross-sectional median → column median. Standardization happens INSIDE each fold (pipeline), never globally. Blocks (34 features total, membership in `artifacts/T3.2/feature_construction.json`):

| Block | Construction | n feats |
|---|---|---|
| OWN | y lags {3,6,12,24}; diffs d1 = y_{t−1}−y_{t−2}, d12 = y_{t−1}−y_{t−13}; Δlog1; momentum sign(d1) | 8 |
| SCSS | starts_all & completions_all: rolling mean/std {12,36} + diff12 | 10 |
| RMS | average_rent_total & vacancy_rate_total: rolling mean/std {12,36} + diff12 | 10 |
| CENSUS | dwelling_value_avg_total & income_median_before_taxes: diff12 + rolling mean {12,36} (annual step series — no std) | 6 |

### Incremental design + decision rule (pre-stated)

lag1-only reference → +OWN → +SCSS → +RMS → ALL, identical folds/masks via `harness.evaluate`; **a block earns T6 candidacy iff mean paired intersection-mask ΔMDA > 0 when added AND full-mask NRMSE does not degrade >10%**; per-fold signs reported honestly.

## 3. VERDICT: NO temporal family carries DIRECTIONAL signal; the OWN block carries a large POINT-ERROR gain

- **The pre-stated keep rule fails for every increment**: all mean paired ΔMDAs are NEGATIVE (≤2/5 folds positive), driven mostly by fold 1 (2008–09) where extra features hurt most. Under the letter of the rule, nothing advances to T6 candidacy on directional grounds.
- **But the point-error story is real and sizable**: adding just the OWN block cuts ridge NRMSE by 22% (0.00169 → 0.00131, ratio 0.778, also visible in NMSE/NMAE) while leaving MDA roughly intact. SCSS/RMS/Census blocks add essentially NOTHING incrementally (NRMSE flat within noise, MDA slightly worse) — housing-quantity and rent covariates' rolling histories carry no incremental monthly signal once the CMA's own index history is present.
- Consistent with the near-martingale finding of T2.1/T2.2: more input history sharpens LEVEL predictions but does not beat momentum on direction; if anything it dilutes it (ridge spreads weight onto longer lags whose direction content is stale).
- Every variant remains well below persistence on MDA (best 0.593 vs 0.6771); persistence is still the bar.

### Recommended T6 forward-selection candidates

1. **OWN block, point-error screening**: `tot_d12`, `tot_lag12`, `tot_lag24`, `tot_dlog1` (long-horizon own-index memory) — admit ONLY under the multi-metric bar (any MDA-neutral-or-better subset that keeps NRMSE ≤ lag1-only wins; a >10% NRMSE gain is worth having even at MDA parity since persistence already owns direction).
2. **k=5 neighbour-lag spatial features** (T3.1 corrected: mean ΔMDA +0.0100, NRMSE ratio 0.999) — marginal keep, low priority.
3. **DROP**: k=3 spatial features (corrected mean ΔMDA −0.0009), SCSS/RMS/Census engineered blocks (no incremental metric improvement of any kind).

## Pitfalls encountered

- First logging pass produced a registry row with a SPACE in its id (`T3.2-intersect-all blocks-vs-lag1only`); fixed the id generation and re-appended the clean row — per convention the latest timestamp wins; the space-id row is a harmless draft.
- The static-transform approach requires the train-split file to contain the validation-window years (it does: folds carve val windows out of X_train_full_total.csv, 1981–2022) — documented above; per-fold REFITTING of transforms would be equivalent here since shifts are parameter-free, but the T3.3 leakage audit must re-verify this under train-only re-imputation.
- Fold-1 dominance of the ΔMDA degradation: 2008–09 sits right before the financial-crash regime shift; regularized models lean harder on stale long lags exactly when momentum breaks. Not chased further (α tuning is T5's job).
