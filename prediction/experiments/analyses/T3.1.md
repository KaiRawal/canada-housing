## 2. The spatial-feature experiment

### Design

- **Geography:** representative-point centroids of all 24 study CMAs from the upstream Census-2021 boundary shapefile (`data_cleaning/data/cleaned/cma_boundary/lcma000b21a_e.shp`, EPSG:3347 → WGS84; explicit name mapping in `spatial_features.CMA_TO_SHAPE`, all 24 matched incl. both Ottawa-Gatineau parts). Great-circle distances. Caveat: 2021 boundaries applied to the 1981–2022 panel (benign for kNN weights).
- **Features** (k = 3 and k = 5 variants, 4 columns each): neighbour mean lag-1 level, neighbour mean lag-1 Δ, plus inverse-distance-weighted versions of both.
  **Info-set argument:** features are pure backward shifts of neighbours' OBSERVED actuals (`y_{j,t−1}`, `y_{j,t−1}−y_{j,t−2}`) — exactly the information persistence / ridge-on-lags / recursive variants already condition on for the CMA's OWN series. No contemporaneous or future values enter. Late-starter CMAs (~2016-12, no train history in folds 1–4) get valid features because their NEIGHBOURS have histories; only each CMA's own first two panel months lack lags (576/400 NaN cells of 31 168/31 168 → 1.8%/1.3%, filled 0.0). Neighbour weights renormalise over available neighbours per month.
- **Runs:** temporal-only ridge (reference block) vs ridge+neighbour-lags, identical folds/masks via `harness.evaluate`; persistence reported alongside by the harness itself.
- **DECISION RULE (pre-stated):** keep iff paired intersection-mask ΔMDA (spatial − temporal-only) > 0 on a majority of folds AND mean paired ΔMDA > 0 AND full-mask aggregate NRMSE ≤ 1.10× temporal-only.
- Optional LightGBM probe SKIPPED (lightgbm not installed; not warranted for an ablation slot).

## 3. VERDICT: marginal KEEP into T6 ablation — definitively NOT a direction fix

> **Correction (T3.2):** this section's "both k=3 and k=5" verdict predates the
> paired-scorer anchor fix; see §2b — after correction, k=3 FAILS the rule
> (mean ΔMDA −0.0009 → DROP) and only k=5 remains a marginal keep.

Both k=3 and k=5 satisfy the letter of the pre-stated rule (ΔMDA > 0 on 3/5 folds; mean +0.0006 / +0.0077; full-mask NRMSE ratios 0.983 / 0.985 — slightly BETTER point errors), so the feature family survives INTO T6 ablation screening rather than being killed outright. But the honest reading:

- effects are tiny (≤ +0.008 mean paired ΔMDA against fold noise of ±0.01–0.02) and inconsistent across folds;
- both models remain ~0.06–0.07 MDA below persistence (0.604 / 0.613 vs 0.6771);
- neighbour levels are highly collinear with own lag-1 (corr 0.48–0.56); neighbour Δs nearly orthogonal but weakly informative (corr 0.01–0.07 with own lag-1 level);
- consistent with T2.3's LOCO verdict: cross-CMA signal adds almost nothing once own-history lags are present.

Recommendation for T6: treat neighbour-lag features as a low-priority ablation family (k=5 preferred over k=3 if screened at all); do NOT expect them to close the gap to persistence.

## Pitfalls encountered

- First feature implementation aligned different CMAs' row indexes directly (pandas union → silent all-NaN columns, caught only because every metric came out bit-identical across variants); rewritten around date-pivoted wide panels with per-month weight renormalisation.
- The `--no-log` smoke run exposed a gap where the paired-intersection registry rows were logged unconditionally — fixed, but its rows remain in runs.jsonl (append-only policy): `T3.1-intersect-*` appear 3× each and `T2.3-intersect-*` 2× each with distinct timestamps; dedupe by `(run_id, timestamp)` per the standing convention.
- Intersection-masked NRMSE is computed on flat-excluded rows and is NOT comparable to full-mask NRMSE across studies (initially inflated the degradation ratio to 1.88 before switching to like-for-like full-mask aggregates).
- geopandas centroid extraction needed `.astype(float)` after transposing the dict-of-dicts frame (object dtype broke the haversine ufunc).
- Registry-hygiene caveat (noted as a convention in T3.2 critic fix #3): when a run_id appears multiple times in runs.jsonl, the EARLIEST duplicates may be broken drafts of that run — e.g. the bit-identical k3/k5 rows @09:53 were the all-NaN first-draft feature build — so downstream tooling must treat the LATEST timestamp per run_id as authoritative.
