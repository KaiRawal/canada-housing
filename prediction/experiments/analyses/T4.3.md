### 2. New experiment — recursive vs direct vs classical

**Folds & features exactly as T4.1/4.2**: 5 expanding 2-year folds 2008-09 … 2016-17 (train rows 5624→7352, 18 CMAs folds 1-4, 24 in fold 5; val windows 24 months each = 432 rows folds 1-4, **323 n_val_rows in fold 5 pre-mask (24 CMAs × partial histories), 317 direct eval rows after common-mask (late-starter gaps), and 245 classical eval rows (6 late-starters excluded — see below)** — documented per-fold in `*_results.json` `folds[].n_val_rows` vs `per_fold[].n_eval_rows` and `artifacts/T4.3/combo_table.csv` note). Feature sets pruned to A/B only (per 2026-08-28): `A_base_lag1` (176 lag-1 cols), `B_base_lag1_own_sub` (A + 4 OWN-subset: `tot_d12`, `tot_lag12`, `tot_lag24`, `tot_dlog1`). C/k5 and SCSS/RMS already proven C≈B and dropped.

**Inner-CV discipline identical**: expanding time-ordered blocks WITHIN each outer train window ONLY (4×12m), no shuffle. Winning pipeline refit on full outer train window. Implemented via manual loop (clone + sequential fits) to avoid joblib deadlocks.

**Recursive framing**: `skforecast` is not installed in this environment (checked `import skforecast` → `ModuleNotFoundError`; `versions.skforecast = not_installed_fallback_manual_recursion`). Manual recursion fallback used and documented: train 1-step pooled LightGBM (and Ridge) on train window with actual features, then forecast 24 months iteratively per CMA — OWN-subset features `lag12/24`, `d12`, `dlog1` are recomputed from the *predicted* history (causal recursion: `lag12 = hist[-12]`, `d12 = hist[-1]-hist[-13]`, `dlog1 = log(hist[-1])-log(hist[-2])`, 0 fallback when insufficient history), while the 176 covariate lag-1 columns use the **observed val row's actual lag-1 covariate values** (partial recursion — isolates target-memory recursion effect; future covariate values beyond horizon not needed because they are lag-1). For A (no OWN) recursion is a no-op → predictions identical to direct (verified bit-identical). Documented as caveat: only OWN-subset is truly recursed; covariates remain observed.

**Classical**: per-CMA `SARIMAX(1,1,0)` (statsmodels, `enforce_stationarity=False`) fit on train window only per CMA (≥10 obs required), h-step forecast where h = n val rows for that CMA in the fold (24 for full-history CMAs). **6 late-starter CMAs have insufficient history and are excluded from classical scoring → n_eval_rows fold 5 = 245 for SARIMAX/VAR vs 317 for direct/recursive; baseline on classical denominator is 0.67205±0.0395 (245-row) vs direct baseline 0.67712±0.0474 (317-row)** — see `artifacts/T4.3/combo_table.csv` denominator note and `T4.3-SARIMAX-110-A_results.json` `agg.baseline`. Pooled `VAR(1)` attempted on the wide month×CMA target panel (train months only, `pivot` + `ffill/bfill`, require n_obs > n_vars); graceful fallback to NaN predictions if infeasible — it **did** fit in this run (wide panel 197 months × 18 CMAs, VAR(1) succeeded; forecast mapped back via Period index). Both classical models are fit per-fold train window only, never seeing val.

**Scoring**: all models through `harness.evaluate` (or equivalent `_score_fold` recursion path) on canonical folds. Direct/recursive models scored on **identical masks per fold (common rows where model+baseline both present, 317 rows fold 5)**; persistence scored alongside on identical masks — paired intersection-mask deltas are exact. **Classical models are NOT on identical masks to direct/persistence in fold 5 (245 vs 317 rows, 6 CMAs missing) → their Δ vs global persistence is agg-delta only, not paired common-mask; their paired Δ is vs own baseline (245-row denominator, ΔMDA −0.0039) — see combo table footnote. Per-fold `paired_deltas.json` not regenerated for recursive because recursive vs direct deltas are direct differences on same mask (A: 0, B: see table); classical paired deltas reported vs own baseline.**

## Interpretation

1. **Recursion does not unlock directional skill beyond the direct linear/GBM ceiling.** The best recursive model (recursive-LGBM-B, 0.5942) still trails persistence by 0.083 MDA and nominally trails B-enet by 0.004, with worse point errors. The observed +0.017 MDA lift of recursive over direct LGBM-B is within noise (0.038 std) and is direction-only — point errors do not improve (GBM-safe tie). For the canonical B-enet winner (0.5981), recursion is not applicable (linear direct already uses actual lag history); recursive GBM does not surpass it. Ridge recursion actively harms point errors (2×).

2. **Near-martingale panel quantifies remaining headroom.** Even the per-CMA SARIMAX(1,1,0) — a pure time-series model that *should* excel at 1..24-step recursion — only ties persistence directionally and loses badly on point errors (NRMSE 0.00785 vs 0.00113) when scored on its own 245-row denominator. This confirms the T4.1/4.2 verdict: monthly NHPI `total` has ~40% flat directions and is near-martingale; multi-step iteration accumulates error without gaining direction.

3. **Classical models fail the multi-metric bar.** SARIMAX's nominal MDA proximity to persistence (+0.070 vs B-enet direction) comes with 7× point-error blow-up; VAR(1) is worse on both. They do not provide a headroom path for T4.4 leaderboard — direct B-enet remains the T4 frontrunner for the final model.

4. **Pruned scope validated.** A/B only (B = A + OWN-subset) was sufficient — C/k5 already C≈B, and recursion on A is provably no-op. No SCSS/RMS needed.

## Pitfalls encountered

- **VAR feasibility**: pooled VAR on 18 CMAs × ~197 months is borderline (n_obs > n_vars+lag); `ffill/bfill` for late-starter gaps was required; without it VAR fails with `LinAlgError`. Documented as "gracefully fails on short panel" but in this run it succeeded — still uncompetitive.
- **Fold-5 val row count**: **323 is `folds[].n_val_rows` pre-common-mask (24 CMAs × partial histories for 2016-17, including months where some CMAs have NaN target), 317 is `per_fold[].n_eval_rows` for direct/recursive/persistence after common-mask (rows where both model and baseline have non-NaN predictions/targets), and 245 is `per_fold[].n_eval_rows` for classical (6 late-starter CMAs excluded entirely due to <10 train obs).** Direct-LGBM / recursive paths use identical 317-row masks, so their comparison remains apples-to-apples; classical vs global persistence is agg-only. Per-fold `n_eval_rows` and `n_mda_directions` are in the saved JSONs.
- **Covariate lag-1 handling in recursion**: full recursion would require forecasting covariates too; we isolate the effect to target-memory (OWN-subset) by using observed covariate lags for val months — **partial recursion caveat**: 176 covariate lag-1 columns remain observed, only OWN-subset (tot_d12/lag12/24/dlog1) recomputed from predicted history. Documented in `decision_rule.json:caveats`.
- **Ridge-OWN harm**: recursive-Ridge-B 2× NRMSE blow-up (0.00373→0.00750, fold5 0.01347→0.03198) shows linear recursion accumulates error; LGBM-B tie (0.00139→0.00140) shows GBM is more stable but still no improvement.
